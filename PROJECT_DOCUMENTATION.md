# **Requirements Outline**
### <u>Functional Requirements</u>
**Purpose of the Application**

This website is designed to promote the series through the multiple different sections of information by captivating the audience in the amazing world of Wings of Fire. Each character, book and arc can be related to the reader themselves and intrigues them to learn more, as well as engaging with fans because of the large fanbase that enjoy the series.

**Uses Cases**

The user can use the side menu bar to navigate to the topic they want (e.g "Series"). Users can scroll through the different sections and choose one (e.g. "Arc 2, Book 9: Talons of Power"). The user can then chose to browse the other books in the arc (Arc 2), or to learn more about the specific protagonist of that book ("Turtle"). Once users had read through the character's introduction, they can browse all the other protagonists by swiping left or right, return to learn more about the books (scroll up), or navigate anywhere else using the sidebar navigation menu.



**Test Cases**
\
Test Case 1: When the user selects ‘Series’, the application will direct them to the ‘Series' menu, where they may browse the multiple different arcs and side stories of the series,
Testing: Self-Testing
Test Case 2: When the user selects a book, the website will direct them to the official blurb of the book, where they may also browse the other books in the series.
Testing: Self-Testing
Test Case 3: When the user clicks on a character's page, the website will direct them to a small introduction to the character's personality, beliefs and a bit of history (no spoilers), and they may browse the other character's information.
Testing: Self-Testing
Test Case 4: When the user selects “Map", the website will direct them to the uncoloured Pyrrhian map, where they may also browse the coloured Pyrrhian map and the coloured and uncoloured Pantalan map.
Testing: Self-Testing


### <u>Non-Functional Requirements</u>

**Performance**\
The application will be fast and smooth, with the loading time between screens occurring within one second.

**Usability**\
The application will have large readable text with button outlines and bright colours for easy accessibility.

**Reliability**\
The application will be tested across multiple devices and tested to ensure it is consistent and bug free.

**Security**\
The app does not collect any security data, as it does not include any login data or user feedback forms.

# **Social, Ethical and Legal Issues**
### <u>Social Impact</u>
* **Target Audience Considerations**\
This application is available for the larger majority of the audience, and is created with accessibilities in mind. There are large texts, simple controls and an easily accessible side menu bar to help with navigation.
* **Potential Benefits**\
This project positively encourages users to read through the whole series, as it is one huge amazing story broken into arcs and books, each focusing on each character’s journey through obstacles and internal struggles. It will encourage fans to discuss how vast the setting is, with its two continents Pyrrhia and Pantala. It brings attention to the detail Tui. T. Sutherland put into the series for the best immersion for each book, a great topic for fans to talk about, fostering the community of fans for Wings of Fire.
* **Potential Risks**\
Since the book’s setting is in a fantasy world where the story and protagonists are dragons, the designs do not include misrepresentation or exclusion of groups, as the seven dragon tribes all look similar to their respective environment. The book contains some themes in the book of war, family abuse and corrupted ideas for power, but none will not be included in this website, as they are deeply integrated into the story.

### <u>Ethical Responsibilities</u>
* **User Data & Privacy**\
The prototype will not collect any user data, as it is a simple website to give access of information to others.
* **Representation & Inclusion**\
This project accurately represents the characters, themes and ideas from the series without any misgenderisation or exclusion. I only use official artwork that was commissioned by the author in m website to avoid any misunderstandings.

* **Content Sensitivity**\
The book does contain controversial topics such as violence and discrimination but the prototype will not include these because it will website that just informs the reader about the setting and characters,, so there could be no controversial topics involved.

### <u>Legal Considerations</u>
* **Copyright & Intellectual Property**\
All the artworks used in my website are official art, which will be credited in large world for a clear understanding.

* **Terms of Use**\
If I were to launch this website, it would fall under 'fair use' as it only uses official Wings of Fire content for a non-commercial reason. It is purely for fan engagement and information purposes without any way t earn money. If the creators ad publishes ask to remove or adjust content, I will do so immediately as they are the right holders.
